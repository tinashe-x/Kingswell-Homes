About us

Kingswell Homes & Hospitality

At Kingswell Homes & Hospitality, we believe that accommodation should feel comfortable, welcoming and easy.

Our properties are designed for guests who want more than a traditional hotel room. With separate living spaces and fully equipped kitchens, our self-catering accommodation gives you the freedom and privacy to enjoy your stay at your own pace.

We welcome guests travelling for business, leisure, family visits, relocation, contractors and longer stays. We take pride in providing well-presented accommodation with the essentials you need for a comfortable stay.

Our accommodation

Everything you need for a comfortable stay

Our self-catering accommodation gives you the freedom to enjoy your stay your way — with comfortable bedrooms, smart TVs, fully equipped kitchens, private bathrooms, fresh hotel-quality linen and free Wi-Fi.

Bedrooms & bedding

Beds are prepared with fresh, hotel-quality linen, with towels provided for guests.

Living areas

Relax in a comfortable lounge with sofas and seating, with smart TVs available for your entertainment.

Fully equipped kitchen

Stove, fridge, microwave and kettle, together with cookware, utensils, plates, glassware and basic kitchen supplies.

Bathroom

Wi-Fi & utilities

Laundry
